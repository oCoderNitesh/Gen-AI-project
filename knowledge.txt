Parala Maharaja Engineering College, commonly known as PMEC, is an engineering college located in Berhampur, Odisha.
PMEC offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs.
The Computer Science and Engineering department focuses on programming, software development, artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science and related technologies.
Students can participate in technical events, hackathons, workshops, internships and other academic activities.
The college encourages students to develop technical skills through practical projects and extracurricular activities.
